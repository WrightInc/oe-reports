Part 1 — Demo · Version 1

One inbox runs almost everything

Today, almost the entire company's job flow moves through one inbox: dispatch@oeservices.ca. Every Customer email lands there — and all six yards must read through everything to find what's theirs, even email addressed directly to them.

Version 1 is helping improve the management of this. It reads every email that arrives, and sends a copy to the right yard automatically — GTA, Durham, Hamilton, Barrie, London and Ottawa.

IF THE SENDER IS AN OE EMAIL ADDRESS

Nothing happens — the email is delivered exactly as addressed. The OE team member already knows who they're sending it to. Not routed.

IF THE SENDER IS A CUSTOMER OR ANYONE OUTSIDE OE

They are checked against OE's routing list. A copy is sent to whoever OE has assigned — one person or several.

IF THE SENDER ISN'T IN THE ROUTING LIST

The email is delivered as normal. New senders can be added to the list in seconds.

The system never decides on its own where email goes. It only follows the rules the OE team provides — who to route, and to whom. Nothing is assumed or made up. If OE hasn't set a rule, the email will continue to flow as normal.
NOTHING ASSUMED. NOTHING MADE UP.

The actual flow — running today

This is the actual flow, running in Microsoft Power Automate today, with the capability to significantly expand upon this.

The routing list

The routing list is one Excel file in SharePoint — simple to update. Any change takes effect automatically, with nothing further required. And the routing rules themselves can be changed at any time, based on how the OE team wants to receive their email.

A Customer / Team Portal — one platform for everyone

Beyond dispatch, the same AI foundation makes a Customer / Team Portal possible — one secure, OE-branded platform, built as part of OE's website. Customers see everything about their jobs; OE's team gets its own Team Hub. Permissions decide who sees what — and because it's one platform, every update is made once and appears in the right place automatically.

Delivered in two phases: Phase 1 (Q4 2026) — the Team Hub, including the automated Safety Messaging HR has requested; Phase 2 (Q4 2027) — the Customer-facing portal. Because it's one platform, Phase 1 already advances Phase 2.

Built mobile-first
Around 80% of OE's team work from a tablet or phone, so the Team Hub is designed for the field first: fast, simple, made for a small screen — while working just as well at a desk.
