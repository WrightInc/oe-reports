Weeks 1–2
Project Kick-off and Governance Setup. Formal project initiation meeting with OE leadership; project steering committee established.
Weeks 3–5
Internal Capacity & Sentiment Assessment. Five structured, anonymous interviews with a cross-section of employees; internal baseline survey distributed.
Week 8
Leadership Alignment Session & Phase 1 Deliverable. Audit findings presented to the Leadership team; Current State Assessment and Strategic Alignment Report submitted.
Weeks 13–16
Onboarding & HR Integration. Onboarding presentations rewritten; Manager's Toolkit developed to support Indigenous employee retention.
Weeks 6–7
Documentation and Policy Audit. Review of existing human resources policies and onboarding materials; parent-company Indigenous policies reviewed for scalability.
Weeks 9–12
Core Education Curriculum Development. Multi-module training program designed for field crews; Field Protocol guides created for crews working in or near Indigenous communities.
Weeks 17–22
Pilot Training & Yard Rollout. Virtual pilot training sessions at two select yards; full rollout of core training across all seven OE Utility Services locations.
Weeks 23–24
Integrity & Recognition Framework. Calendar established for recognizing important dates; internal program designed to recognize employees advancing the roadmap's goals.
Weeks 25–28
KPI and Measurement Framework Setup. Key Performance Indicators defined to track progress in staff education and internal engagement; reporting dashboard established for the Board.
Weeks 29–32
Internal Governance Formalization. Internal Indigenous Relations Working Group formalized with representatives from different parts of the business; terms of reference and meeting cadence defined.
Weeks 33–35
Annual Review and Reporting. End-of-year survey measures shifts in workplace culture against the Phase 1 baseline; annual progress report template drafted.
Week 36
Project Closeout and Roadmap Handover. Final roadmap and measurable outcomes presented to the Board of Directors.
